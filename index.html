PART ONE

Understand the Pattern

Discover What’s Really Keeping You Stuck

Before you can change the pattern, you have to recognize it.

  • Recognize your imposter pattern—including how self-doubt can hide behind achievement, preparation and high standards.
  • Identify what’s driving it so you can separate old experiences and internal narratives from what is true about you now.
  • See what it’s costing you in opportunities, visibility, decisions and the way you show up.
  • Catch it in real time so the pattern stops operating unnoticed.
PART TWO

Change How You Respond

Build the Confidence and Clarity to Make Your Next Move

Understanding the pattern isn’t enough. The next step is changing your response to it.

  • Interrupt the pattern before it makes the decision for you.
  • Own what you bring to the table without minimizing, apologizing or constantly proving yourself.
  • Show up with greater confidence and visibility even when you don’t feel 100% certain.
  • Make your next move by identifying what you’ve been postponing and deciding what happens next.
