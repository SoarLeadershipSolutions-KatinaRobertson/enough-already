01

Day One — Understand the Pattern

September 16 · 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM ET

Discover What’s Really Keeping You Stuck

Before you can change the pattern, you have to recognize it.

By the end of Day One, you’ll be able to:

  1. Recognize your imposter pattern — including the ways self-doubt can hide behind achievement, preparation and high standards.
  2. Identify what’s driving it — so you can begin separating old experiences, expectations and internal narratives from what is actually true about you now.
  3. See what it’s costing you — in opportunities, visibility, decisions and the way you show up.
  4. Catch it in real time — so the pattern stops operating unnoticed.

You won’t leave Day One simply knowing more about imposter syndrome. You’ll understand how it operates in YOU.

02

Day Two — Change How You Respond to It

September 17 · 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM ET

Build the Confidence and Clarity to Make Your Next Move

Understanding the pattern isn’t enough. Day Two is about changing your response to it.

By the end of Day Two, you’ll be able to:

  1. Interrupt the patterns that make you second-guess yourself before they make the decision for you.
  2. Own the evidence of what you bring to the table without minimizing, apologizing or constantly trying to prove yourself.
  3. Show up with greater confidence and visibility even when you don’t feel 100% certain.
  4. Identify the move you’ve been postponing and determine what you’re going to do next.

You’ll leave with a personal action plan for what happens after the event—not just pages of notes.
